Editorial & Ephemeris Queries

If you observe a planetary coordinate discrepancy compared to standard ephemerides such as the Indian Astronomical Ephemeris or Jagannatha Hora, please include:

  • Exact Date of Birth (YYYY-MM-DD)
  • Exact Time of Birth (HH:MM in 24h format)
  • Exact City and Geographic Coordinates
  • Selected Ayanamsa standard (Lahiri, Raman, or KP)
