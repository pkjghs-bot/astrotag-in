Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

At Astrotag (accessible at https://astrotag.in), accessible via our web application and associated domains, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected, stored, and utilized by Astrotag.

1. Stateless Architecture & Astrological Data

Astrotag operates on a privacy-first, stateless session architecture. When you enter your name, date of birth, time of birth, and place of birth into our Vedic Kundali calculator:

  • Your personal birth data is processed ephemeral in-memory to compute planetary coordinates and divisional charts.
  • We do not require account registration, passwords, or personal email addresses to generate birth charts.
  • Astrotag does not store or sell your astrological profiles or birth data to any third-party marketing entities.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Cookies

We use third-party advertising companies, primarily Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on Astrotag.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

3. Log Files & Analytics

Like many other web services, Astrotag uses standard web hosting log files provided by our hosting partners (Vercel and Cloudflare). The information inside the log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamp, referring/exit pages, and number of clicks. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information and are used solely for analyzing trends, defending against automated denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, and maintaining platform uptime.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Astrotag does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that our service holds personal information of a child, please contact us immediately and we will promptly purge such records.

5. GDPR & California Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

Under GDPR and CCPA/CPRA, you are entitled to certain consumer rights, including the right to request disclosure of categories and specific pieces of personal data collected, request deletion, and opt-out of data sales. Because Astrotag operates statelessly without persistent user tracking or selling data, no consumer profiles are traded or commodified.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please contact us via our Contact Page or email us at support@astrotag.in.
